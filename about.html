About

My background is in data science and cognitive science, and I'm currently pursuing a master's in data science at the University of Colorado Boulder. I'm particularly interested in artificial intelligence, embodied cognition, and adaptive systems, especially how intelligent behavior emerges through interaction with an environment.

I currently work on applied AI systems for clinical and rehabilitation settings, including speech and language modeling. Alongside this, I'm developing independent research projects examining how agents learn from self-generated experience and whether attention-based architectures can support the emergence of sensorimotor habits.

I also write about technology, cognition, and their broader economic and institutional contexts.
